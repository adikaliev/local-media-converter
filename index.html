Good to know

        
    
          
  • Private: everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.
    • 
          
  • 
            First use: the FFmpeg engine (about 9 MB) downloads from jsDelivr or unpkg, then your browser caches it.
            Networks that block both CDNs can't use this page.
          
    • 
          
  • 
            Speed: conversion runs on a single CPU core, so it is much slower than desktop apps.
            In one test, re-encoding a 20-second 720p clip took about 2 minutes, so long videos can take hours.
            Fast copy and audio-only output usually take seconds, and the Fast encoding speed helps with MP4 and MKV.
            Keep this tab open until it finishes.
          
    • 
          
  • 
            Size: the result has to fit in browser memory (2 GB at most, less on phones), so very large files may fail.
          
    • 
          
  • 
            Fast copy is almost instant and lossless, but cuts happen at the nearest keyframe (the result can start a few seconds early),
            and it fails when the original codecs don't fit the chosen format. MKV accepts almost anything.
          
    • 
          
  • Tracks: only the main video and audio track are kept; subtitles and extra audio tracks are dropped.
    • 
          
  • HDR / 10-bit video is converted to standard 8-bit color and can look washed out.
    • 
          
  • Browsers: works best in a recent desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
